# User Checklist for 'Networks Decide for Us' Paper

Please provide the following items to facilitate the paper writing process.

## 1. Bibliography
- [ ] **BibTeX File (`references.bib`)**: A complete BibTeX file containing all the references mentioned in the report and any other relevant literature.

## 2. Figures & Tables
Please export these as high-resolution PDFs or PNGs and place them in `paper-NDFU/figures/`.

- [ ] **Network Visualization**: A visual representation of the calculated "plausible networks" (ATP/GSS) vs. Random Networks.
- [ ] **Heatmaps (Results)**:
    - [ ] Average Final Adoption by (Gamma, h) for Random Seeds.
    - [ ] Breakdown: Rational Adoption vs. Social Adoption vs. Total Adoption.
    - [ ] Heatmaps showing Phase Transitions.
- [ ] **Regression Tables**:

- [ ] **Seeding Strategy Plots**:
    - [ ] Comparison of Adoption curves/bars for Central vs. Marginal seeds.
